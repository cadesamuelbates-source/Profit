## Recommendation (provisional)

**Contract-to-Close Desk** is a concierge-first service for agents who close 12+ sides a year and
run their own files. You check every deadline against the signed contract, then deliver a client
timeline page, calendar invites, and a reminder schedule, at **$129 per file**.

It wins on customer access and on money already being spent: human transaction coordinators charge
$300–500 per file and AI tools charge about $15. It doesn't win on novel software, because
competitors exist. Roblox (median creator payout $1,440/yr) and a trading bot on your own capital
(needs millions to earn $10k/month even with a real edge) are not recommended as the business bet.
Details: [docs/03-decision.md](docs/03-decision.md).

## Verified
