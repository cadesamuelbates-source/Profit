# First 10 paying customers: channels and exact messages

**Status: drafts. Nothing has been sent.** Sending any of these needs your approval, your broker's
OK (see the offer checklist), and the state rule profile in place.

## Channels, in order

| # | Channel | Why it should work | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Personal text to agents you already know** who close 12+ sides | Warmest trust. They'll tell you the truth. | 15 contacts |
| 2 | **Referrals from every conversation**: "Who else closes a lot and still runs their own files?" | Busy agents know other busy agents | 10 contacts |
| 3 | **Lender and title reps you work with**: ask for introductions only | They see which agents' files are chaotic, and smoother closings help them. *Don't pay or accept referral fees.* | 10 contacts |
| 4 | **Five minutes at your office meeting** (with your broker's permission) | One conversation reaches 10–30 agents | 1–2 meetings |
| 5 | Local association or market Facebook groups | Only after 3 paying customers, as social proof. Follow each group's rules. | later |

Pull your list from MLS production data or your brokerage roster: agents with 12+ closed sides
in the last 12 months. Record where each contact came from in `tracker.csv` (`source`).

## Message 1: text to an agent you know

> Hey {first name}, quick one. I'm testing a service for agents who run their own files: send me
> a signed contract and same day I'll send back every deadline checked against the contract, a
> clean timeline page for your client, and calendar invites. Reminders go to lender, title, and
> the other agent. $129 a file, and you only pay if it's useful. Do you have a deal under contract
> right now that I could set up as a test? Happy to show you a sample first.

> how your last file went? Ten minutes is plenty.
>
> {Your name} · {phone}

## Message 3: 60 seconds at an office meeting

> "Quick show of hands: who had a deadline get uncomfortably close on a file this year? … I'm
> testing a service for agents who run their own files. You send me the signed contract, and same
> day you get every date checked against it, a timeline page your client can actually read, and
> reminders that go to lender, title, and the other agent. $129 a file, pay only if it helped.
> I'm looking for five agents with a deal under contract right now. Grab me after."

## Follow-up (once, 3–4 days later, then stop)

> Hi {first name}, following up once on the contract-to-close setup. If timing's bad, no
> worries. If you have a file going under contract in the next few weeks, I'm happy to set it up.

## Words to avoid

Don't say "guarantee," "never miss a deadline," "legal," "compliant," or "AI does it all."
The promise is narrower and true: *checked dates, a clear client page, and timely nudges, with the
contract governing.*
