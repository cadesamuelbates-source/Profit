# Contract-to-Close Desk: founding offer (draft; your name and state go in the blanks)

**For agents who close a lot of deals and still run their own files.**

Send me the fully signed contract. Within 2 business hours I'll deliver:

- **A checked deadline schedule.** Every date is computed from your contract's terms and then
  compared line by line with the signed contract and addenda. You get the checklist.

  they need to do and by when, who to call, and a wire-fraud warning.
- **Calendar invites** for you and your client, with alerts the day before and two hours before
  each deadline.
- **Reminder schedule.** Your client, the lender, title, and the other agent get nudged before
  deadlines they own. You get a short digest two days ahead. (During the pilot, reminders go by
  email from ________ with you copied. Texting starts only after consent and carrier registration.)

doesn't replace your judgment. The signed contract always governs. You stay the agent of record and
decide what goes to your client.
