# Open questions and provisional assumptions

## The three questions that change the plan

1. **Your real-estate role and reach.** What do you do: licensed agent, broker, transaction
   coordinator, lender, property manager, or investor? In which state and metro? About how many
   agents who close **12 or more deals a year** could you message personally this week?
2. **Time and money.** How many hours a week can you commit for the next 90 days? How much cash
   are you willing to put at risk?
3. **Skills.** How much have you coded: none, scripts, or a shipped app? Do you have any game-design
   or trading-system experience?

Why these three: question 1 decides whether the real-estate advantage is real and which state's
contract rules apply. Question 2 sets how fast the plan can move and whether delivery has to be
outsourced early. Question 3 decides how much of the build you can maintain yourself.
