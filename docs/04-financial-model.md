⚠ means the sales hours needed just to replace churned agents exceed the founder's assumed capacity. Sales isn't outsourced in this model, so that row isn't achievable as specified. Conservative economics need a different channel, not more effort.

**Reading it:** in the base case, $10k/month before founder pay requires about 106 agents each sending about one file a month. To hold that level you need about 5.3 new paying agents every month, just to replace churn. The ramp in section 2 shows founder-led sales at 5 new agents a month plateaus near 100 agents. So the target needs a channel that scales: brokerage or team deals, referral loops, or partnerships. A founder working alone, one agent at a time, won't get there. That's why the experiment measures repeat use and sales hours per agent, not just a first payment.

## 5. Experiment arithmetic (why 4 paying agents out of 40)

If each qualified agent independently pays with probability p, the chance of seeing at least 4 payers among 40 contacts is:

| True paid-conversion p | P(≥4 of 40) = CONTINUE signal | P(0 of 40) = STOP signal |
|---|---|---|
| 2% | 1% | 45% |
| 5% | 14% | 13% |
| 10% | 58% | 1% |
| 15% | 87% | 0% |
| 20% | 97% | 0% |
| 30% | 100% | 0% |

The rule rarely passes a weak offer (p ≤ 5%) and usually passes a strong one (p ≥ 15%). Results of 1–3 payers mean ITERATE. Those are your *warmest* contacts. Cold conversion is usually lower, so if warm conversion is under 5%, reaching ~100 paying agents would take thousands of cold contacts in a single market.

**Trading your own capital.** A bot is only worth what it earns *above* a passive benchmark you could hold for free. Capital needed for $120k/year of excess return:

| Excess return over benchmark (after costs) | Capital needed |
|---|---|
| 1% per year | $12,000,000 |
| 3% per year | $4,000,000 |
| 5% per year | $2,400,000 |
| 10% per year | $1,200,000 |

Evidence on retail day traders (97% of persistent Brazilian futures day traders lost money) points to excess returns at or below zero. Without substantial capital this isn't an income path, even if it works.

**Trading-journal SaaS.** At $29/month with 3.5% payment fees, 30% affiliate commission (a common channel in trading tools, ESTIMATE) and ~$300/month fixed costs, you need ≈ **534 paying subscribers**. At 10% monthly churn (ESTIMATE) that means ≈ 53 new subscribers every month, in a market where TradeZella, TraderSync, Edgewonk and Tradervue already compete on price.

**Property-tax appeals (contingency).** At $250 average fee per successful appeal (Ownwell's own worked example is $250; real averages vary by county), a 60% success rate and $40 to acquire each client (HYPOTHESES), $120k/year needs ≈ **1,091 signed homeowners a year**, almost all inside a short seasonal window, in a state where you're allowed to do it.
